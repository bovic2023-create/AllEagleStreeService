Tree Pruning Service<h2>
Pruning targets the structure of the tree, not just its edges. We remove crossing branches that wound each other, co-dominant leaders competing for dominance at the crown, and dead wood that creates drop hazards over time. Every cut is made just outside the branch collar so the tree can seal the wound on its own terms.
%%
Tree Root Pruning<h2>
Roots follow water and space. When a tree grows near a driveway, foundation, or sewer line, the roots follow those pathways with no awareness of the damage they are causing. Root pruning severs specific lateral roots at a calculated depth, redirecting growth before structural or plumbing damage escalates. Done correctly, it protects your property without compromising the tree.

Frequently Asked Questions<h2>
<list>
What is the difference between tree trimming and tree pruning? Trimming generally refers to cutting back overgrown growth for clearance, aesthetics, or size management. Pruning is more targeted: it focuses on the tree's internal structure, removing specific branches to improve health, reduce hazard, or correct the tree's long-term form. In practice, both involve cutting, but the goal and technique are different. A trim cleans up the edges. A prune changes the architecture. All Eagles crews assess which approach your tree actually needs before starting.^^
How do I know if my tree needs trimming, pruning, or removal? Start with what you are noticing. Overgrown branches blocking gutters or windows usually need trimming. Crossing branches, dead wood, or a canopy that looks unbalanced usually need pruning. A tree showing significant structural decay, major lean, or more than half its canopy dead is likely a removal conversation. When you call All Eagles, we walk your property and give you a straight answer based on what we see, not what generates the most work.^^
Can tree roots really damage my foundation or pipes? Yes, and it happens gradually enough that most homeowners do not notice until the damage is done. Roots grow toward moisture. Foundations and sewer pipes both accumulate moisture around their perimeters, which makes them attractive targets for opportunistic root growth. Small cracks in pipes or foundations give roots a pathway in, and once inside, root pressure widens those cracks over time. Root pruning, installed correctly and at the right depth, creates a physical boundary that redirects growth before it reaches critical infrastructure.^^
What should I do immediately after a storm damages a tree on my property? Stay away from the tree until you know its status. If any part of it is in contact with your home or a power line, treat the situation as an active hazard and keep people at a safe distance. Do not attempt to remove debris that is in contact with the damaged section. Call (888) 322-8516. We assess the situation before any cutting begins, because a storm-damaged tree under tension behaves unpredictably and needs a trained eye before it gets a chainsaw.
