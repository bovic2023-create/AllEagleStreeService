How #State Soil Shapes Tree Health From the Ground Up<h2>
The soil profile across much of #State creates specific conditions that homeowners rarely think about until a tree fails. Compacted soils in established neighborhoods reduce oxygen availability around root systems, which stresses the tree over time even when the canopy looks perfectly healthy above ground. Poor drainage in lower-lying #State properties creates chronic root saturation, which weakens root structure and accelerates rot at the base.
All Eagles crews assess soil conditions as part of every site visit in #State. We look at surface drainage, compaction near the root zone, construction history that may have disrupted soil structure, and signs of root stress in the surrounding lawn. What we find below the surface often tells a more accurate story than what we can see in the canopy above.
